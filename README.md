# L3ad.io IOU Method

Open notes for how [L3ad.io](https://l3ad.io) scores Rising businesses.

**Canonical method page:** https://l3ad.io/method  
**Site:** https://l3ad.io

L3ad.io is Florida's unsponsored business directory. Nothing on the lists is for sale. No sponsored spots, paid badges, featured tiers, or lead fees.

## What this repo is

A public mirror of the published method so anyone can inspect the rules used for Rising lists. When scoring code and list data ship with each Wednesday issue, they land here too.

## Method · Version 1 (draft)

Every list on L3ad.io follows these rules. If a rule changes, the version number changes and the old version stays published.

### Who is eligible

A business can appear on a Rising list only if all of these are true:

1. It holds an active Florida license for the trade, confirmed in the state's public license records.
2. That license was first issued within the last five years.
3. It has no final disciplinary order on that license in the state's published records.
4. Its license or business address is in one of the counties covered for that metro.

Complaints that never reached a finding are not used. Florida keeps them confidential, and an unproven complaint is not evidence.

### What we look at

- **License record.** Status, license type, first issue date, and any published discipline.
- **Business record.** Entity status and formation date from the state business registry.
- **Website transparency.** Whether the business shows its license number, a real address, what it offers, warranty terms, and a way to reach a person.
- **Public footprint.** Whether the business is active and reachable today.

### What never counts

- Money. No business can pay to be listed, to rank, or to be removed.
- Advertising spend, search rankings, or social following.
- How long a business has existed, beyond the five-year cutoff for Rising.
- Whether a business has contacted us.

### Opinion, not a certification

Each Top 5 is editorial opinion formed by applying this method to public records. It is not a guarantee, a license check you should rely on, or an endorsement. Always confirm a license yourself before you hire.
